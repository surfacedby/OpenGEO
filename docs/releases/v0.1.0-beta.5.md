# OpenGEO 0.1.0-beta.5

This release makes the journey from questions to answers to useful next steps easier to follow.

## What's new

- **Topics.** Group prompts around a product, service or customer need, then run and review that group separately. Thanks to Hudson for the original contribution, along with lighter workspace polling and saved AI search queries.
- **Prompts with context.** Review AI suggestions, revisit earlier suggestion batches and open the answers for each tracked prompt. The AI searches tab shows only queries actually reported by the provider.
- **Checks you can follow.** Progress stays visible as you move around the app. Starting the same check twice is prevented. ChatGPT and OpenRouter checks can prepare recommendations within the same approved budget, with saved answers available while analysis continues.
- **Gradual website discovery.** Let OpenGEO work through your public pages in the background. It saves progress between batches, respects crawl rules and waits when a website asks it to slow down. Pages are grouped by type or folder, with their checks and evidence together.
- **Clearer comparisons.** Explore competitors and references by platform or website type, compare cited answers, and open the prompts and pages behind the counts. Classification quotes remain available for review.
- **Better evidence views.** Sources has website, page and citation-context views. Responses has separate answer, source and search tabs. Drawers open flush to the edge and protect unsaved changes when dismissed.
- **More relevant recommendations.** Reviews use complete saved answers, consider the stored page catalog before proposing new content, and keep page edits separate from new resources and website changes.
- **Security and reliability.** Patched dependencies, durable crawl cooldowns, clearer update errors and fixes to page-content extraction.

## Install or upgrade

Choose the Windows, Apple Silicon or Intel Mac download. Follow the [installation guide](../distribution.md) and compare its hash with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Export a workspace backup before upgrading.

Windows can download updates and install them after confirmation. Mac can download a verified disk image; replacing the app in Applications remains manual. Automatic downloads can be disabled for a metered connection. The Windows Finish screen offers choices to open the app and create a desktop shortcut.

## Beta scope

Windows builds are unsigned. Mac builds are ad hoc signed and unnotarized; upgrading can require macOS Keychain approval for saved connections. These packages do not claim publisher verification or clean-machine certification. The release manifest records the exact packages and completed platform checks.

AI providers have separate eligibility, limits and costs. No provider is connected automatically, and missing answers or unreported searches stay missing. Optional usage sharing can be disabled in Settings. Managed supplier output, billing, refunds, activation and hosted imports remain separate service acceptance gates; this desktop beta does not certify those services.
