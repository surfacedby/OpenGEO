# OpenGEO 0.1.0-beta.6

Ask one question and compare the answers, without starting a full visibility check.

## What's new

- **Ask from any workspace screen.** Type in the header, choose your engines and read their answers side by side. Thanks to Hudson for the original contribution.
- **Keep useful questions.** Search your Ask history and add a question to tracked prompts or a Topic. One-off answers stay separate from visibility measurements; future checks collect their own answers.
- **Follow work across the app.** Ask progress and paused work remain visible when you switch screens. Each answer shows its sources, reported searches and collection surface.
- **Safer retries.** A lost reply can be retried without creating a second Ask. The original engines, spending approval, brand and language stay attached to that request.
- **Clearer costs.** ChatGPT uses your connected plan. Paid engines require an explicit choice and share your spending approval. Estimates, recorded charges and unresolved cancellations are distinguished. DataForSEO's actual metered charge can differ from its estimate.
- **More consistent presentation.** Shared drawers support outside-click dismissal, keyboard focus and full-height layouts. Topic headings, prompt indentation, metric icons and answer typography make the hierarchy easier to scan. Unreviewed competitors are no longer presented as zero discoveries.
- **CLI and MCP support.** Ask, read answers and track useful questions from the existing automation interfaces.

## Connections and availability

Ask uses only the engines available on your connection. SurfacedBy selected-question access is separate from full checks; OpenGEO does not buy a full check when that access is unavailable. This release does not enable accounts, add unsupported managed platforms or change service prices. A full-check price is not a single-question quote.

## Install or upgrade

Choose the Windows, Apple Silicon or Intel Mac download. Follow the [installation guide](../distribution.md), compare its hash with `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and export a workspace backup before upgrading.

Project exports include tracked checks and drafts. One-off Ask history remains on the original device and is not included in those exports.

Windows can download updates and install them after confirmation. Mac downloads a verified disk image; replacing the app in Applications remains manual. Automatic downloads can be disabled for a metered connection. The Windows Finish screen offers choices to open the app and create a desktop shortcut.

## Beta scope

Windows builds are unsigned. Mac builds are ad hoc signed and unnotarized; upgrading can require macOS Keychain approval for saved connections. These packages do not claim publisher verification or clean-machine certification. The release manifest records the exact packages and completed platform checks.

Projects stay on your computer. Optional usage sharing can be disabled in Settings. AI services have their own eligibility, usage limits and charges; web search availability depends on the selected service. Missing answers and unreported searches remain missing. Managed output, billing, refunds, activation and hosted imports remain separate service acceptance gates.
